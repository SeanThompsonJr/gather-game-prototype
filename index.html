Gather & Game Events: Pull up a chair.

Gather & Game brings tabletop RPGs, console gaming and tournaments to anime and pop culture conventions in Florida. Roll for initiative, grab a controller, or meet your next player two.

Every kind of player has a seat

First-timers, lifelong dungeon crawlers and anime fighting game fans all end up at the same table. Here's what we set up at every con.

Tabletop adventures

One-shot stories run by our game masters. Sit down as strangers, leave as a party. New players are welcome and we teach as we go.

  • Daggerheart
  • Marvel Multiverse RPG

  • Tomb of the Unknown

Tournaments

Bracket play with a crowd behind you. Our Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game bracket packed the casual area at Ocala Comic Con.
